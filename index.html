Garlic Ginger Fried Rice

Day-old rice, a hot wok, and a triple-garlic hit — the fried rice that ruins takeout for you.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 3 cups day-old cooked rice
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a wok over high heat until shimmering.
  2. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 20 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Push aside, scramble in eggs until just set.
  4. Add rice, breaking up clumps, tossing to coat for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Stir in soy sauce, toss another minute, top with spring onions.

Pad Thai

Sweet, sour, and salty in perfect balance — the wok-tossed noodle classic done right at home.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 200g flat rice noodles
  • 200g shrimp or tofu
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tbsp palm sugar
  • Bean sprouts, crushed peanuts, lime wedges

Steps

  1. Soak rice noodles in warm water until pliable, drain.
  2. Stir-fry shrimp or tofu in hot oil until nearly done, push aside.
  3. Scramble in eggs, then add noodles and sauce mixture.
  4. Toss until noodles absorb the sauce, fold in bean sprouts.
  5. Top with crushed peanuts and a squeeze of lime.

Sticky Mango Rice

Coconut-sweetened sticky rice and ripe mango — Thailand's favorite dessert, made simple.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 cup glutinous rice, soaked overnight
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 ripe mangoes, sliced
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Steam soaked rice for 20–25 minutes until tender.
  2. Warm coconut milk with sugar and salt until dissolved.
  3. Fold most of the coconut milk into the hot rice, rest 10 minutes.
  4. Plate rice with sliced mango, drizzle remaining coconut milk.
  5. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds.

Aglio e Olio Pasta

Garlic, olive oil, and chili flakes — proof that four ingredients can be a masterpiece.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp red chili flakes
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Parmesan, to serve

Steps

  1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente, reserve 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Gently fry garlic in olive oil until golden, not browned.
  3. Add chili flakes, then toss in drained pasta.
  4. Loosen with pasta water until glossy, stir in parsley.
  5. Finish with grated parmesan.

Margherita Pizza

Blistered crust, fresh mozzarella, and basil — the pizza that started it all.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza dough ball
  • 1/2 cup crushed tomatoes
  • 125g fresh mozzarella, torn
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven with a pizza stone or tray to its highest setting.
  2. Stretch dough into a round, spread with crushed tomatoes.
  3. Scatter mozzarella evenly over the sauce.
  4. Bake 8–10 minutes until the crust blisters and cheese bubbles.
  5. Top with fresh basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

Mediterranean Lentil Soup

Hearty red lentils, cumin, and lemon — the soup that tastes like it simmered all day.

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
  • 6 cups vegetable stock
  • Juice of 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Sauté onion and carrots in olive oil until soft.
  2. Stir in cumin and paprika, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add lentils and stock, simmer 20–25 minutes until tender.
  4. Blend partially for a thicker texture, if desired.
  5. Finish with lemon juice before serving.
